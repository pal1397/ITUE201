ABOUT STUDENTHUB

"Know more about our platform"

WHO WE ARE

StudentHub is a college portal that helps students manage their academics, attendance, fees, events, clubs and profile — all at one place.

OUR MISSION

To make student life simpler by bringing all academic and administrative services together on a single, easy-to-use platform.

WHAT WE OFFER

  • 🎓 Student Dashboard & Profile
  • 📚 Academics Hub (Courses, Subjects, Timetable, Attendance, Result, Study Material)
  • 🎉 Events & Clubs Participation
  • 💳 Fees Management & Online Payment
  • ⚙️ Admin Control Panel for Institute Management
